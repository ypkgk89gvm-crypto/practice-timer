Parent settings

Sound is checked live on this iPad to see if an instrument is playing. Nothing is recorded, saved or uploaded; only minutes are logged.

Higher = soft playing counts. Lower = only clear playing counts (if talking or room noise is counting, turn it down).

The bar should jump past the line when someone plays, and stay left of it when the room is quiet.
